The Garden of Forking Paths

To Victoria Ocampo

In his A History of the World War, Captain Liddell Hart reports that a planned offensive had to be postponed because of torrential rain.

Before the sun set on this same day I ran the same risk. Madden was implacable and I knew the end of all our work was near.

I went up to my bedroom closed and locked the door and waited on my narrow iron bed beneath the hazy six o'clock sun.

In ten minutes I had developed my plan. The telephone directory gave me the name of the one person capable of passing on the information.

The road kept descending and branching off through meadows misty in the twilight while a high pitched music moved with the breeze.

We sat down and spoke of labyrinths of time and of infinite futures diverging and converging in a web of possibility.
